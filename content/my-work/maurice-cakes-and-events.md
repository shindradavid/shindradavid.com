## Project Overview

Maurice Cakes & Events is a full-service bakery and events company offering custom cakes, event decoration services, and professional baking training. I developed a comprehensive digital platform that combines a customer-facing e-commerce experience with a powerful backend ERP system to streamline their entire business operations.

## Key Components

### E-commerce Website
A user-friendly online storefront that allows customers to:
- Browse and order custom cakes for various events
- Schedule deliveries to most parts of the world
- Explore event decoration services for weddings, introductions (kukyaala), baby showers, birthdays, and more
- Learn about the Maurice School of Baking and Decoration

### ERP System
An integrated business management solution that handles:
- **Order Management**: Track customer orders from placement through fulfillment
- **Finance Management**: Monitor revenue, expenses, and financial reporting
- **Inventory Control**: Manage baking ingredients and event decoration supplies
- **Customer Relations**: Maintain customer records and order history
- **Event Scheduling**: Coordinate event decoration bookings and staff assignments

### Baking School Portal
A dedicated section showcasing the Maurice School of Baking and Decoration, featuring hands-on training programs with expert instructors:
- Baking Level 1 & 2
- Advanced Baking and Cake Decoration (Master Class)
- Events Decoration Level 1
- Events Decoration & Management Level 2 & 3

The complete platform enables Maurice Cakes & Events to efficiently manage their diverse service offerings while providing customers with a seamless digital experience.
