## Project Overview

SCZ Cakes is a multi-location cake business offering a wide variety of custom cakes for all occasions. I developed a comprehensive digital ecosystem that combines a web and mobile shopping experience with a sophisticated multi-branch ERP system to manage operations across all their locations.

## Key Components

### E-commerce Website
A modern, responsive web platform where customers can:
- Browse the latest cake collections and seasonal offerings
- Place orders for pickup or delivery
- Create customer accounts to track order history
- Connect via social media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X)

### Mobile Application
A dedicated mobile app providing:
- Convenient on-the-go cake ordering
- Push notifications for order updates and promotions
- Easy access to branch locations and contact information
- Seamless user experience optimized for mobile devices

### Multi-Branch ERP System
A centralized enterprise resource planning solution featuring:

**Branch Management**
- Oversee multiple branch locations from a single dashboard
- Monitor branch-specific performance and operations
- Manage staff and resources across all locations

**Order Management**
- Track orders from placement to fulfillment
- Route orders to the appropriate branch based on location
- Handle custom cake requests and special instructions

**Sales & Finance**
- Real-time sales tracking across all branches
- Consolidated financial reporting
- Revenue analysis by branch and product category
- Expense management and cost tracking per location

**Inventory Control**
- Monitor ingredient stock levels at each branch
- Automated reorder alerts
- Transfer stock between locations as needed

The integrated platform gives SCZ Cakes complete visibility and control over their entire business, enabling efficient scaling and consistent customer experiences across all branches.
